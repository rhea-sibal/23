The Promise

Rhea,

If I could go back and fix every moment I fell short, I would. But I can't. What I can do is try to be better from here.

I know I've lost your trust. And I know that doesn't just come back because I built something nice. But I want you to know that I'm willing to earn it back. Slowly. Bit by bit. However long it takes.

I miss you, Rhea. I miss us. I know we had our differences and I know I was wrong. I'm not making excuses for any of it. But I don't want to lose the friend I have in you. I really don't.

I want to start fresh. Not pretend like nothing happened, but actually do things right this time. Show up when it matters. Be someone you can count on again.

I think about all the small things now. How much you love your coffee, your Heytea, those little moments that light up your day. I didn't get it before. I didn't understand that those things weren't small to you, they were everything. And I'm sorry I ever made you feel like they didn't matter. No more of that. Ever. I promise.

I don't care about where the future takes us. I really don't. What I care about is right now. The present. And right now, you're what matters to me the most.

Happy 23rd birthday, Bunny. I hope this year gives you everything you deserve, which is way more than I could ever fit into words or a website.

Nishit
